Reading in Two Languages at Once

Keeping the original text next to a translation lets you check the meaning without losing the author's voice.

Why keep the original?

A translation is always an interpretation. When the source sentence stays on the page, you can compare word choices, spot technical terms that should stay as they are, and return to the original wording whenever something feels off.

A few habits that help

  • Read the English sentence first, then glance at the translation.
  • Mark unfamiliar terms and look them up in context.
  • Turn the translation off when you want to practice on your own.
